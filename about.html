Project Overview

This research project addresses the critical challenge of cost-effectively optimising the balance between scaling renewable energy resources, sizing storage capacity, and managing the precise timing of on-farm energy consumption. Building upon a pilot study funded by AgriSearch in collaboration with Dale Farm, the Ulster Farmers’ Union (UFU), and CAFRE, this PhD research aims to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI) techniques to mitigate environmental impact. By maximising the utilisation of on-farm renewable generation and intelligently offsetting grid dependency, the framework seeks to minimise the overall kilowatt-hour (kWh) cost per litre of milk produced, thereby enhancing the long-term sustainability and competitiveness of Northern Ireland’s dairy sector.

Methodology & Engagement

To construct data-driven, empirical digital twins, the study aims to incorporate comprehensive data collection across both traditional and robotic dairy farm configurations. This system-level modelling relies on high-fidelity operational parameters to simulate and advise on optimal renewable energy configurations. The initial phases of this framework's development have been driven by comprehensive regional data collection, mapping current infrastructure, energy tariffs, and baseline technology adoption across the sector.
